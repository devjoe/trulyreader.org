Early Preview Feedback

Truly is an early preview. Your feedback helps shape what it becomes.

The early preview feedback form will be linked here before the first public release. For now, you can contact hello@trulyreader.org.

What We Want To Learn

  • What feels useful, confusing, unnecessary, or wrong.
  • What you want Truly to help with next.
  • Whether you want to join a tester group.
  • Whether suspected fake accounts or coordinated influence comments are a problem for you, and how you usually judge them.

Before You Send Feedback

Please do not include other people's personal data, private posts, or non-public content.

If you have a reproducible bug, visible UI problem, screenshot, or log, please use the public GitHub Issues page after the repository opens.
